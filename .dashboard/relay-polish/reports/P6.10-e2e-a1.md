## Technical Details

The composer toolbar layout (line 286-345 in `src/components/relay/RelayComposer.tsx`) follows this DOM order:
1. PromptInputTextarea (relay-composer)
2. RelayPicker trigger (relay-picker-trigger)
3. ComposerPills (if any — empty in these tests)
4. MicButton (relay-mic)
5. PromptInputSubmit (relay-send/relay-stop)

The Tab order follows DOM order, so reaching the mic requires two Tabs from the composer. The ComposerPills uses CSS `order-first` to position visually above the toolbar, but remains in the middle of the tab order.
